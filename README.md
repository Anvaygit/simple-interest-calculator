# Simple Interest Calculator

## Description

The Simple Interest Calculator calculates the simple interest and total amount based on the principal amount, rate of interest, and time period.

## Formula

**Simple Interest (SI):**

$$
SI = \frac{P \times R \times T}{100}
$$

Where:

* **P** = Principal amount
* **R** = Rate of interest per year
* **T** = Time period in years

**Total Amount:**

$$
A = P + SI
$$

## Example

If:

* Principal = ₹10,000
* Rate = 5% per year
* Time = 2 years

Then:

**Simple Interest = ₹1,000**

**Total Amount = ₹11,000**

## Features

* Calculates simple interest easily.
* Calculates the total amount.
* Simple and easy-to-understand implementation.
* Takes principal, rate, and time as inputs.
